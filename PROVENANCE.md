The Pocket pilot narrows deployment to two public primary sources: SEC Form 4
and USAspending contract actions. It adds evidence-preserving classification,
append-only correction history, conservative entity aliases, and controlled
single-writer DuckDB access. It does not claim authorship of the upstream
collectors or other datasets.
